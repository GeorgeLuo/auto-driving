Objective

Determine the smallest operator-useful and reusable perception-memory feature slice that can be expressed through CLI-useful sequences, then deliver it in a long-lived local workbench page. The CLI and workbench use the same authoritative server-side sequence implementation and structured state; the browser presents the feature without reimplementing its business logic. A bounded audit of the relevant M007 sequences, current CLI capabilities, and existing CLI-launched pages may select existing candidates or define a limited number of new sequences when they are independently useful from both the CLI and the workbench. The exact invocation boundary and visual form may be learned through operator use. M008 closes with one usable slice and a durable assessment of CLI, sequence, and signal gaps rather than broad scenario coverage or a frontend framework migration.
